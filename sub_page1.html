Customer Collection - Strategy One Pager By Vincent Wong 9 min 555 thumbs up 12 AI is disrupting the customer service management (CSM) space: A considerable portion of Tier 1 and Tier 2 customer requests are now being automatically resolved by AI-powered CS agents. This presents a promising opportunity for Atlassian: As AI technology will reduce the reliance on volume-based support in Tier 1/2 support agents. Historically, this area posed a significant challenge for Atlassian to establish a substantial presence in the field. We anticipate a shift in the significance of tasks: With AI CS agents automating the bulk of Tier 1/2 requests, the remaining tasks will involve more complex issues that require human intervention and collaboration. This is an area where Atlassian and its integrated system of work excel. Today, we are observing a growing trend among digital-first customers who are opting for Jira Service Management (JSM) (despite it not being a comprehensive CSM solution), primarily due to the ability to seamlessly integrate CS with development processes. Atlassian CSM solution - Customer Collection: Recognizing a strategic opportunity, we are recommending the launch of an Atlassian CSM solution (Customer Collection). This solution would combine a cutting-edge AI agent with a ticketing engine supported by Jira, integrating with the overall system of work platform. However, it is crucial to act swiftly as existing players are rapidly closing the gap in AI capabilities.
